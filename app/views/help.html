Quick Actions

From the quick actions tab, you can access three functions:

  • Your most recent transaction. The amount of money involved, the date of the transaction, and the transaction's category.
  • Add a new transaction button.
  • Get access to this help page.

Income and Expense Data

The "Income and Expense Data" category shows four key things: the income pie graph, the expense pie graph, each category's weight, and the income versus expense bar graph.


The income and expense pie graphs analyze each category within their respective divisions and calculate what percentage they contribute. This data is visualized as a pie graph for easy interpretation.


The income versus expense bar graph displays your income and expenses side by side, helping you compare them visually.


You can filter the data to display transactions from the last week, month, year, or all-time using the dropdown menu above the graphs.

Transaction History

The transaction history section presents all your transactions in a structured table. It includes the transaction name, amount, balance after the transaction, date, category, and whether it was an expense or income source. You can also edit or delete transactions.


Deleting a row cannot be undone.


To edit a transaction, click the transaction, modify the fields, and then click the edit button again.


You may also use the search bar and dropdown menu to filter transactions by name or category.
